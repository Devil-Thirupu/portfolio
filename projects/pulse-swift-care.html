The Problem & The Solution

The Challenge

In acute medical emergencies — such as cardiac arrest, strokes, and major vehicular trauma — survival rates drop with every minute of delay.

Traditional emergency systems frequently encounter fragmented coordination: dispatchers manually route ambulances, paramedics have limited channels to send telemetry to doctors, and hospital emergency rooms receive critical patient status only after the ambulance physically arrives at the ER doors.

The Pulse Swift Care Solution

Pulse Swift Care unifies the entire emergency chain into a responsive, real-time web platform. When a critical situation occurs, patients or bystanders trigger an instant emergency alert with precise GPS coordinates.

The platform synchronizes ambulance dispatch with continuous live vital signs telemetry. Hospital receiving teams receive pre-arrival patient streams, diagnostic notes, and arrival estimates, enabling medical staff to prepare specialized treatment before the patient even enters the facility.

Key Features

Built to maximize speed and coordination during life-critical moments.

Real-Time Patient Monitoring

Continuous telemetry streaming of vital physiological indicators so attending medical professionals have instantaneous awareness of patient stability.

Emergency Alert System

One-touch panic dispatch system that instantly broadcasts emergency signals, location data, and medical history to nearby first responders.

Ambulance Coordination

Dynamic dispatch routing and navigation algorithms that assign the optimal emergency vehicle based on distance, traffic, and hospital availability.

Hospital Communication Interface

Dedicated emergency department console relaying incoming patient records and estimated arrival times so medical teams can stage critical care beds beforehand.

// emergency_pipeline

Emergency Workflow

How information flows from emergency trigger to hospital reception.

Step 01

Emergency SOS Trigger

The user or IoT device initiates a panic alert, transmitting GPS coordinates and preliminary medical profiles.

Step 02

Ambulance Dispatch

The closest available ambulance unit is automatically notified and dispatched with optimized turn-by-turn routing.

Step 03

Transit Telemetry

Paramedics log vitals and treatment administered en route, updating the live patient cloud stream.

Step 04

Hospital Pre-Arrival Prep

The receiving hospital reviews patient status, assigns an emergency trauma bay, and prepares specialized equipment before arrival.
