The Problem & The Solution

The Challenge

Navigating personal healthcare records is often stressful and complex. Patients accumulate scattered paper prescriptions, handwritten doctor notes, and clinical lab reports filled with confusing medical jargon.

When patients don't fully understand their test values or dosage instructions, adherence drops and anxiety increases. Furthermore, non-native English speakers frequently lack access to healthcare explanations in their native language or colloquial dialect.

The HealthMate AI Solution

HealthMate AI provides a digital medical companion. Patients simply upload photos or scans of their prescriptions and lab reports. Optical Character Recognition (OCR) converts the text into structured digital records.

Using a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architecture powered by Qwen LLM, the assistant answers patient questions grounded strictly in their actual records — explaining what test results mean, tracking health values over time, and communicating fluently in English, Tamil, and Tanglish.

Main Features

Designed to empower patients and simplify healthcare records.

Medical Report & Prescription Management

A centralized digital repository that organizes clinic visits, prescriptions, and lab tests chronologically with search and filtering.

OCR-Based Text Extraction

Extracts key medical metrics, medicine names, frequencies, and doctor observations from printed reports and handwritten slips.

AI-Powered Health Assistant

Translates complex clinical terminology into clear, accessible language, explaining what parameters like HbA1c or lipid panels mean.

RAG-Based Medical Record QA

Retrieval-Augmented Generation queries the patient's own documents so answers are strictly grounded and cited directly from uploaded reports.

Health Value Tracking

Automatically logs vital indicators over time (blood sugar, cholesterol, blood pressure) to help detect trends and progress.

English, Tamil & Tanglish Support

Enables bilingual communication so regional users can comfortably query their medical records in Tamil or conversational Tanglish.

Nutrition & Dietary Insights

Generates practical dietary and lifestyle recommendations aligned with the patient's diagnosed conditions and lab indicators.

Technologies Used & Their Purpose

How each technology contributes to the HealthMate AI system.

Frontend

React

Powers the reactive user interface, providing smooth document uploads, interactive biomarker charts, and conversational chat flows.

Build Tool

Vite

Ensures lightning-fast local development and produces highly optimized production frontend bundles for quick loading.

Styling

Tailwind CSS

Provides a modern, responsive design system with accessible components tailored for both desktop and mobile viewing.

Backend

Python

Serves as the primary language for OCR document extraction pipelines, NLP vectorization, and machine learning integrations.

API Framework

FastAPI

Provides asynchronous, high-throughput REST API endpoints for multipart file uploads, OCR worker tasks, and streaming LLM responses.

Database ORM

SQLAlchemy

Manages relational database models, patient session histories, structured lab parameter tracking, and document metadata securely.

Extraction

OCR (Optical Character Recognition)

Parses uploaded prescription scans and lab report PDFs to extract clinical text, medical values, and doctor signatures into raw digital format.

Language Model

Qwen LLM

High-efficiency open-weights LLM handling clinical medical reasoning, document summarization, and fluent multilingual synthesis (English & Tamil).

Information Retrieval

RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

Retrieves relevant patient document excerpts before prompting the LLM, preventing medical hallucinations and ensuring strict factual accuracy.

// system_architecture

System Workflow

How data flows from document upload to actionable health insights.

Step 01

Upload & Ingestion

The patient uploads an image or PDF of their prescription or lab report via the React client.

Step 02

OCR Extraction

FastAPI dispatches the document to the OCR engine, isolating text, dosages, and clinical test ranges.

Step 03

Vector Chunking & RAG

Extracted content is indexed into a vector store, enabling semantic retrieval of specific patient history.

Step 04

Qwen LLM Reasoning

The user's question (in English, Tamil, or Tanglish) is paired with retrieved document context and processed by Qwen LLM.

Step 05

Actionable Insights

The patient receives clear, factual explanations, vital trend updates, and practical dietary recommendations.
